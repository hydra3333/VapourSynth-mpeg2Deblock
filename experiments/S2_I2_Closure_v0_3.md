## 4. Cross-decoder reproducibility and descriptive findings

Claude's independent Linux FFmpeg rehearsal of the unmodified NumPy engine and the owner's actual Windows BestSource runs generated **byte-identical copies of all eight numeric CSVs for both clips**: `shifted_label_contrast.csv`, `column_phase.csv`, `row_phase.csv`, `known_bad_offsets.csv`, `seam_counts.csv`, `boundary_detectability.csv`, `frame_gop_uncertainty.csv` and `outer_mb_edge_diagnostics.csv` (the human-readable result text differs only in Windows/Linux line endings). These byte-identical aggregate and per-frame statistics, over nine offsets and at full floating-point precision, are **strong evidence** that BestSource and FFmpeg delivered identical luma for every eligible frame, and that the stride-aware adapter preserved the measured information. **No pixel-by-pixel comparison was performed**, so exact pixel equality is not claimed.

The descriptive M2/M4 boundary-detectability experiment uses indexed seam positions versus known non-seam controls; reported hit rates and AUC are *seam-versus-control proxies*, **not** labelled true visible-blocking detection rates. The reader preserves separate FRAME, FIELD, NONE-SKIPPED-P, NONE-SKIPPED-B, NONE-INTER-CBP0 and other applicable classes; both orientations, QP band, picture type, per-frame/GOP uncertainty and outer edges are described in the CSVs. None of these measurements determined the registration PASS gate or selected a filter. The ratified robust vertical M4 calculation gives original LP 23.79% at a 5% proxy control false-alarm target; Claude's former ~47% preview used a different comparator and is now separately proposed as **M4a**, a future S2-I3 research option only.

## 5. Validation limits - important

- S2-I2 validates **spatial coded-grid correspondence**. It cannot conclusively establish arbitrary temporal identity: Claude showed a one-frame temporal slip in original LP can still pass the spatial gate. Structural picture-type checks provide an independent temporal safeguard, but cannot distinguish two frames with the same picture type merely from the type itself.
- Swapping top/bottom fields also passed the spatial measures in both recordings. Same-field sample access prevents cross-field mixing by these tests, but cannot itself certify field *identity/order*. Record as known limitation; do not claim the spatial gate detected it.
- No filters were applied. S2-I2 makes no claims about whether filtering improves picture quality, about real-edge preservation, or about future NONE/chroma policies.
- Tested sample scope is original LP and LP_blocky; the locked hold-outs were not used for tuning/selection.
